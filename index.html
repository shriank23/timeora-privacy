Privacy Policy

Timeora  ·  Focus, Habit & To‑Do

📋 Overview

Timeora is a focus, habit, to-do and goals app. It has no accounts and no internet access, and it collects no personal data. This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what Android does with it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Effective from first install

🔒 Your Data Stays on Your Phone

Timeora collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot.

Everything you enter — habits, tasks, lists, goals, notes, journal entries, focus history, your name and your settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Timeora never transmits it, and we never receive it.

☁️ Phone Backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android includes Timeora's data in your phone's encrypted backup to your Google account, and restores it when you set up a new phone. This is Android's own backup service: Timeora cannot see or access that backup, and its diagnostic log is left out of it.

📤 Sharing You Choose

Your data leaves the phone only when you send it yourself:

  • Backup file and CSV export — go wherever you pick in the Android share menu (for example Drive, Gmail or WhatsApp). That app's privacy policy applies.
  • Copy backup — puts the backup on the clipboard, where keyboard and clipboard apps can read it.
  • Restore — reads only the backup file you pick or paste.
  • Share an achievement — sends only the badge text you share.

🛂 Permissions Used

Timeora requests the following Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show habit, task and goal reminders and focus/timer alerts. You can turn this off at any time in Android Settings.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM — to fire reminders and timer alarms at the exact time you set.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — to show the timer-finished alarm over the lock screen.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — to ask you, only if you agree, to exempt Timeora from battery optimization so reminders are not delayed.
  • VIBRATE and WAKE_LOCK — for haptic feedback when a timer ends and to keep the screen on during a running session (released when it stops or pauses).
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — for the optional app lock. Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen-lock PIN; Timeora never sees them.

None of these permissions are used to collect, track, or transmit any information about you or your device.

📱 Widget and Spoken Announcements

The home-screen widget shows today's habits and tasks, so anyone who can see your screen can see them. Timer announcements are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone.

🌐 Internet Access

Timeora has no internet permission and does not connect to the internet. Its fonts are bundled with the app, so nothing is downloaded while you use it.

👤 Third‑Party Services

Timeora does not use any third‑party service that collects user data:

  • No Google Analytics / Firebase
  • No Facebook SDK
  • No advertising networks (AdMob, etc.)
  • No crash reporting (Sentry, Crashlytics, etc.)
  • No cloud sync service

Timeora may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating dialog. Google Play handles your rating under Google's privacy policy; Timeora receives nothing from it.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Clear Timeora's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall Timeora — this permanently removes all app data from your phone
  • Turn off or delete your Android backup to remove the backup copy

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request deletion of from our end.

👶 Children's Privacy

Timeora does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app has no accounts, no sign‑in, and no data transmission of any kind.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL and in the app, with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
